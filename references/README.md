# Selected source library

methods/ contains DANE GEIH and poverty-method documentation, including the current poverty 2025 dictionary. literature/ contains the selected academic, grey, institutional and comparison documents. historical_background/ is background, not current policy evidence. legal_context/ is context only; no current legal interpretation is certified here.

notes/ contains unchanged copies of the original team reading cards. Copying them does not adopt every assertion as the new methodology. Interpret published findings through literature_matrix.csv and the research question. Neither authorship nor wording was changed.

Use `references.bib` to cite a source and `literature_matrix.csv` to find its contribution and limits. The registers document source details and the role of the additional policy evidence. The manifests record file locations and fingerprints for provenance. The full texts and reading cards are kept in the local library.

Poverty measurement uses GEIH and official processing; its methodology is a central data-method reference, not a second independent causal study. Official income imputations do not turn the burden estimate into an official DANE affordability statistic.
